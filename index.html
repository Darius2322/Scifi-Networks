Terms & Conditions

By subscribing to SciFi Networks you agree to these terms. Contact us on WhatsApp with any questions.

📶 Service Availability

▼
SciFi Networks provides internet services in designated coverage areas within Nyamira County and Kisii Town. Service availability depends on infrastructure reach at time of subscription. We do not guarantee service outside our current coverage map. Customers should confirm coverage at their specific location before subscribing. Coverage is subject to expansion and may change over time.

🔧 Installation Policies

▼
Professional installation is performed by our certified technicians. For standard residential and commercial connections, installation is provided free of charge. For hotspot partnerships, the property owner or manager agrees to provide electricity supply and physical security for SciFi Networks routers in exchange for complimentary device access. Customers must provide safe access to the installation site. SciFi Networks is not responsible for property damage caused by customer-created obstructions during installation.

👤 Customer Responsibilities

▼
Customers are responsible for maintaining and protecting SciFi Networks equipment on their premises. Loss or damage due to negligence, theft or misuse will be charged to the customer at replacement value. Customers must ensure a safe and stable power supply for network equipment. Sharing of commercial connections to third parties without written permission from SciFi Networks is prohibited.

📊 Fair Usage Policy

▼
SciFi Networks operates a Fair Usage Policy (FUP). Customers who consistently consume excessive bandwidth during peak hours may experience temporary speed management to maintain quality for all users on the network. We do not support illegal downloading, torrenting of copyrighted material or using our network for harmful, fraudulent or illegal activities.

🔐 Privacy Policy

▼
SciFi Networks collects customer information solely to provide and improve our services. We do not sell personal data to third parties. We may share data with regulatory authorities if legally required to do so. Customers may request access to or deletion of their personal data by contacting us on WhatsApp. All data is stored securely and protected against unauthorized access.

💳 Billing Terms

▼
Services are billed on a monthly prepaid basis. Payment is due at the beginning of each billing cycle. Accepted payment methods include M-Pesa and other methods communicated by SciFi Networks. Failure to pay within 7 days of the due date may result in service suspension. All prices are in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and are inclusive of applicable taxes. SciFi Networks reserves the right to adjust pricing with 30 days advance notice to customers.

⏸️ Service Suspension

▼
SciFi Networks may suspend service for: non-payment beyond the grace period, violation of fair usage policy, breach of these terms and conditions, or illegal use of the network. Customers will be notified before suspension where possible. Service can be restored upon resolution of the cause. Planned maintenance suspensions will be communicated with reasonable advance notice to affected customers.

❌ Cancellation Policy

▼
Customers may cancel their subscription at any time by contacting SciFi Networks via WhatsApp or phone. Cancellations are effective at the end of the current billing cycle — no refunds are issued for unused days in a prepaid period. Equipment provided by SciFi Networks must be returned in good condition upon cancellation. Failure to return equipment will result in replacement value charges. Re-connection after cancellation is subject to availability and may include a re-installation fee.
